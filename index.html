About Us

15 Years of Transforming Students into Toppers

RS Vignan Academy has been the go-to coaching institute in Hyderabad since 2010. Located in Tarnaka, we specialise in preparing students for India's most competitive entrance examinations — IIT-JEE, NEET, EAMCET (EAPCET), and BITSAT.

Our professional faculty is highly knowledgeable and supportive, helping students excel not just academically but holistically. We offer highly specialised courses that cater to individual learning needs, backed by extracurricular activities for overall development.

  • ✅ Regular mock tests & doubt sessions
  • ✅ Clean, comfortable learning environment
  • ✅ No extra fees — transparent pricing
